# 2.1 Health, Damage & Test Dummies: Implementation Plan

> Status: **planned (2026-10-02).** Parent: [implementation-plan.md](../implementation-plan.md) step 2.1.

## Goal

Abilities get something to hit before they exist. This step builds the receiving end of combat: health, hit shapes, damage, and a training yard with dummies. The ability framework (2.2) is the first thing that deals damage.

Phase 2 was reordered for this in the interview: health and damage moved ahead of the ability framework (old 2.4 → 2.1), so every ability from 2.2 on is tried against real targets. Enemy AI stays where it is (Phase 3). Enemy attacks should be cast through the 2.2 ability framework, so building enemies first would mean hand-coding their attacks twice.

When it's done:

- a new **Training yard** scene: an open room with a few pillars, a static dummy and a patrolling dummy
- dummies have health, an in-world health bar, and show floating damage numbers when hit
- a dummy never dies: damage takes it down to 0 at most, and it refills to full after a few seconds without being hit
- hit shapes (circle, cone, line) and `applyDamage` are a tested, Babylon-free API for 2.2's abilities to call

Out of scope:

- **anything that deals damage.** No debug hit tool, no placeholder swipe. Damage is first tested in play when abilities land (2.2). In 2.1 it's covered by unit tests. Editing a dummy's health in the entity pane moves its bar and starts the refill. A raw edit isn't a damage event, though, so damage numbers are first seen in play with 2.2's first ability.
- **death**, of anything: health stops at 0 and nothing happens. Enemy death comes with the enemy framework (3.1), player death and restart with 3.5.
- **player health:** only dummies have health for now. The player gets it when something can hit them (3.x).
- **the attacking dummy:** it joins the yard once enemies can attack (Phase 3).
- **cover:** hit shapes are pure area checks, not blocked by obstacles. Abilities that need blocking (projectiles, beams) do it themselves with the collision casts (2.2 / 2.5).
- **mover-vs-mover collision:** the patrolling dummy walks through the player. Unit separation and collision come with enemy steering (3.4).
- hit flash, hitstop, shake, knockback and polished damage numbers (2.7). 2.1's numbers are plain and functional.

### Defaults I picked (object if you disagree)

- **New folder `src/combat/`** (Babylon-free, deterministic, added to rules 1 and 6 in AGENTS.md):
  - `shapes.ts`, the hit shapes on the ground plane:
    - `circle { center, radius }`
    - `cone { origin, dir, range, halfAngle }`
    - `line { origin, dir, length, halfWidth }`, a rectangle from the origin. Abilities will make it a pill (round ends) if they want.
    - `hits(shape, hurtbox)` tests against a target's hurtbox, so **touching the body counts**, League-style, not the center. Hurtboxes are footprints (`collision/body.ts`): a circle, or a pill as a row of circles (the tiger's `bodyRadius`/`bodyLength`). So the player's hit pill fits without new code once they have health.
    - `targetsIn(world, shape)` lists the entities with `health` + `hurtbox` that the shape touches, in a stable order (entity insertion order), for determinism.
  - `damage.ts`: `applyDamage(world, target, amount, source)`. It clamps health at 0, and pushes a damage event.
- **Components (`ecs/world.ts`), plain data:**
  - `health { current, max }`
  - `hurtbox: Footprint`, the body's shape for hits, separate from the movement circle (like 1.6's pill)
  - `dummy { kind: "static" | "patrol", sinceHit, lastHealth }`. The dummy system resets `sinceHit` (seconds, advanced by `dt`, rule 5) whenever health is below `lastHealth`. So any drop counts as a hit (damage or an entity-pane edit), and `applyDamage` knows nothing about dummies.
  - `patrol { a, b, towardB }`
- **Damage events: a per-tick queue.** A single `events` entity holds `combat: CombatEvent[]` (`{ kind: "damage", target, amount, at }`, where `target` is an entity id and `at` a position). The snapshot system clears it at the start of each tick, systems and `applyDamage` push onto it, and the view reads it in `onTick`. Juice (2.7), the HUD (2.6) and enemy reactions (3.x) read the same queue. Entity ids: check how the entity picker and replay checksums identify entities (`world.id(entity)`?) and use that. The id must survive snapshots, or the event can't reference it.
- **Systems:**
  - `patrolSystem`: steers `mover.desired` toward the current end at the dummy's speed, and flips at arrival, braking like the player's click-to-move (√(2·decel·d)).
  - `dummySystem`: notices drops, advances `sinceHit`, and refills to full after `dummy.refillDelay`.
  - Both run in the yard's system list before `locomotion`.
  - `movementStats(entity)` returns the dummy's numbers for dummies. That's the per-entity hook it was made for.
- **Tunables `dummy`:**
  - `maxHealth` 1000
  - `refillDelay` 3 s
  - `patrolSpeed` 2 m/s, with the player's accel/decel (or its own if it feels off)
  - `hurtRadius` 0.45 m
  - `collideRadius`, the static dummy's obstacle circle, ~0.35 m
- **Room data:** `Room` gets an optional `dummies: DummySpawn[]` (`{ kind: "static", at }` or `{ kind: "patrol", a, b }`). `spawnRoom` adds them. The static dummy also adds a `circle` obstacle (type `"dummy"` or `"pillar"`, see the steps) so nav and collision treat it like a pillar. New room `data/rooms/yard.ts`: the player spawns south, the static dummy at the center north, and the patrol runs east–west across the middle (~8 m). Scene id `yard`, label "Training yard", and it's added to `rooms.test.ts`'s random walks.
- **View (`scenes/yard.view.ts`, or the shared room view plus a dummies module in `render/dummies.ts`):**
  - **Model:** the training dummy glTF through `pnpm models:build` and `render/models.ts`. It's static (no skeleton). The patrolling one turns to face its direction and gets a slight bob or sway so it doesn't glide like a chess piece. It casts and receives moon shadows. The hitboxes switch shows a grey-box capsule instead.
  - **Health bar:** a billboarded bar in the overlay scene over each dummy (a dark backing, a fill colored by tokens, a lagging "recent damage" chip that drains after a moment). It's always shown on dummies.
  - **Damage numbers:** one per damage event. It pops at the hit point above the dummy, rises and fades over ~0.8 s on `viewTime()`, so pause, seek and time scale behave. It's a pooled `DynamicTexture` plane in the overlay scene. Plain style. 2.7 polishes it (crits, stacking, juice).
  - The `footprint` debug category also draws dummy hurtboxes. A new `hits` category draws each shape that `targetsIn` tested, with nothing in 2.1 but ready for 2.2.
- **Model search:** I look for two or three CC0/CC-BY training dummy models (Poly Haven, Sketchfab CC, Quaternius-level detail or better, never low-poly), show them to you with screenshots, and only then add one to `assets-src/` + `build-models.ts` + CREDITS.md.
- **Tests:**
  - `combat/shapes.test.ts`: each shape against circle and pill hurtboxes, with edges grazing, inside, behind the cone and past the line's end. Cone angles near 180°. Rotation invariance.
  - `combat/damage.test.ts`: clamps at 0, events, multiple hits in one tick, and the queue cleared next tick.
  - `dummy.test.ts`: refills only after the delay since the *last* hit, and doesn't assume `dt`.
  - `patrol.test.ts`: reaches both ends, turns back, and stays on the segment.
  - Replay fixtures: the yard gets one (walk around and through the patrol), since health isn't touched by input yet.
- **cf bump:** per AGENTS.md, bump `cf` to the latest beta at the start of the step. Any friction goes in a log here.

## Architecture

```
combat/                (new, Babylon-free)
  shapes.ts            HitShape, hits(shape, hurtbox), targetsIn(world, shape)      (tested)
  damage.ts            applyDamage, CombatEvent                                     (tested)
ecs/world.ts           health, hurtbox, dummy, patrol, events
systems/
  dummy.ts             refill after refillDelay                                    (tested)
  patrol.ts            back-and-forth via mover.desired                            (tested)
  snapshot.ts          clears the per-tick combat event queue
  movementStats.ts     per-entity: dummy numbers
data/rooms/
  room.ts              + dummies: DummySpawn[]
  yard.ts              the Training yard
scenes/
  arena.ts             spawnRoom spawns dummies; yardSim
  yard.view.ts / render/dummies.ts   model, health bars, damage numbers
render/models.ts       + the dummy model
```

Rule 1 holds: `combat/` and the new systems are Babylon-free, and only the view reads the event queue.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Training yard is in the scene switcher. It has an open room, a few pillars, a static dummy and a patrolling dummy, with the same atmosphere as the arena.
- [ ] The static dummy blocks the player in both schemes, and moba paths go round it. The patrolling dummy walks its route through the mover systems, brakes and turns at the ends, and faces where it walks.
- [ ] The dummy model is not low-poly, is credited in CREDITS.md, and the hitboxes switch replaces it with a grey-box shape.
- [ ] Lowering a dummy's health in the entity pane drops its bar, and it refills to full after `dummy.refillDelay` without hits. Damage events pop a damage number (checked locally in 2.1, in play in 2.2). Health never goes below 0, and nothing dies.
- [ ] Bars and numbers stay readable through fog and post, and follow pause, frame step, time scale and replay seeks.
- [ ] `combat/` is Babylon-free and deterministic, with tested shapes (circle, cone, line vs circle and pill hurtboxes), `applyDamage` and the event queue.
- [ ] The existing replay fixtures pass unchanged, and the yard has its own. `pnpm check` passes.
- [ ] Docs are updated, the devlog entry is written, 2.1 is ticked, and the PR is open with the checks and Preview green.
