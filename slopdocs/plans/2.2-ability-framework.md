# 2.2 Ability Framework + Cat Auto Attack: Implementation Plan

> Status: **planned (2026-10-02).** Parent: [implementation-plan.md](../implementation-plan.md) step 2.2.

## Goal

The first thing that deals damage, built on the machinery every later ability uses. 2.2 builds the **ability framework** in full: slots, cooldowns, the instant/windup/channel cast states, aiming, and abilities as data. One ability proves it in play: the Cat's **auto attack (AA)**, a single-target melee attack on the Training yard dummies. The framework doesn't know about the player, so enemies cast through it in 3.1.

Naming: it's the **auto attack** (AA), never a "swipe" (a later ability may be called that). An AA is always single target.

When it's done:

- in **WASD**, left-clicking a dummy makes the cat auto-attack it whenever the AA is ready and the dummy is in range. Holding left click while moving attacks whatever enemy the cursor is over, without stopping the movement for longer than the strike.
- in **MOBA**, right-clicking a dummy makes the cat chase it into range and keep attacking it, League-style. Holding right click acts like spamming it under the cursor.
- the AA plays the tiger's attack clip, enemies under the cursor are highlighted with an attack cursor, the current target has a ring under it, and damage numbers pop on the dummies
- the framework covers instant, windup and channelled casts, target/direction/point aiming, and effects as data. Unit tests cover all of it, including casts by a non-player caster.

Out of scope:

- **any ability besides the AA.** Slots `ability1`–`3` and `ultimate` exist but are empty, so their keys do nothing. Pounce comes in 2.4, the first damage ability in 2.5.
- **final keybinds** (2.3). Only the minimum moves: WASD's AA takes left click, so `interact` moves to `F` for now.
- **HUD** and cooldown display (2.6).
- **juice:** hit flash, hitstop, shake, knockback and better damage numbers (2.7).
- **enemy casting, telegraphs and AI** (3.1, 3.2). The framework is entity-agnostic and tested with a non-player caster, but no enemy casts in game yet.
- **death** and **player health** (3.x). Dummies still refill.
- **augments/modifiers** (later), and the loadout screen. The Cat's slots are set in code. Stats are read through one function, so modifiers can hook in later.

## Decisions

| Area | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| What the basic attack is | **An auto attack on `primary`**, defined and cast through the ability framework | Outside the 3 basics + ultimate of the loadout. Always single target. |
| WASD key | **Left click** (was right) | `interact` moves from left click to `F` until 2.3. |
| Targeting (both schemes) | **The enemy you click**, picked on screen | "It's whoever we left click, same as the moba control scheme's right click." No cursor-direction or nearest-enemy picking. |
| WASD target rules | **Sticky target, cleared by movement or another AA command** | Clicking a dummy keeps attacking it (held or released, even with the cursor drifting off) until a WASD movement or a click elsewhere. While left click is held, the enemy under the cursor becomes the target, so holding A + left click moves left, attacks whatever you hover whenever the AA is ready, and keeps moving. Abilities don't clear the target. |
| WASD out of range | **Nothing until in range** | No auto-walk in WASD. The target stays, and the AA fires the moment you walk into range. |
| MOBA target rules | **League** | Right-click an enemy: chase into range, attack, and keep attacking after release until the next order. Right-click the ground: move order. Holding right click = spamming it under the cursor. `S` clears the order. Abilities don't clear it. |
| Hover pick | **Screen space, recorded in the input frame** | The view picks the enemy under the cursor (anywhere on the body, like League). The input frame carries it as `hover`, so it's recorded and replays stay deterministic. The sim never raycasts. |
| Movement during the AA | **Rooted during the windup only** | Moving during the windup is ignored, not a cancel. Free again right after the strike. |
| Windup and range | **Always lands** (League melee) | Once the windup starts, the hit lands on its target whatever it does. |
| Framework scope | **Full framework, only the AA in game** | Slots, cooldowns, all three cast states, three aim kinds, effects as data. Instant and channel are covered by test-only definitions. |
| AA tempo | **0.7 attacks/s** | Base numbers start low and slow, to leave room for upgrades. |
| Visuals | **Attack clip, hover highlight + attack cursor, target ring, debug range ring** | Juice waits for 2.7. |

### Defaults I picked (object if you disagree)

**Cat AA numbers** (tunables group `catAA`):

- `attackSpeed` 0.7 /s, so the period is ≈1.43 s
- `windupShare` 0.18, i.e. a windup of ≈0.26 s. It's a share of the period, so attack-speed upgrades shorten the windup too, like League.
- `range` 1.2 m, edge to edge: from the cat's movement circle to the target's hurtbox
- `damage` 20, so 5 hits empty a 100 HP dummy

**Cooldowns and the input buffer:**

- An ability's cooldown starts when its cast starts, like League's attack timer.
- A pressed ability that can't start yet (on cooldown, or another cast running) is buffered for `abilities.buffer` 0.2 s.
- The AA doesn't need the buffer: it's driven by the target, not by presses.

**Stable entity ids (`uid`):**

- miniplex's `world.id()` is lazy (2.1's notes), so a new `uid` component holds a number from a per-world counter. The counter lives on a singleton `ids: { next }` entity, so snapshots restore it.
- Every spawned entity gets one through a `spawn(world, entity)` helper.
- The AA target, the MOBA attack order and the input frame's `hover` all hold uids.

**`faction: "player" | "enemy"`:** the player is `player`, dummies are `enemy`. Hover and targeting only consider enemies with `health` and a `hurtbox`.

**Abilities as data (`data/abilities.ts`, Babylon-free):** `{ id, aim, cast, cooldown, roots, effects }`

- `aim`:
  - `target` (an enemy uid, range-checked edge to edge)
  - `direction` (a unit vector toward the aim point)
  - `point` (a ground point clamped to range)
  - `self`
- `cast`:
  - `windup` seconds; 0 = instant
  - then an optional `channel { duration, interval }` that applies its effects every interval
  - whether movement cancels a channel is a flag
- `effects`:
  - `damage` on the target
  - `damage` in a hit shape (the 2.1 circle/cone/line) placed relative to the caster and the aim
- Numbers come from a `stats(caster)` function that reads the ability's tunables live, so 2.5's abilities and later modifiers change one place.

**Components:** a generic `caster` component, so enemies can have one too:

- `slots`: primary, ability1–3, ultimate → ability id + cooldown left
- `cast`: the running cast (slot, ability, phase, elapsed, resolved aim) or null
- `target`: the sticky AA target uid or null
- `queued`: the buffered press
- `casts`: a counter so the view can start one animation per cast, like `player.orders`
- the MOBA attack order lives with the move order on `player` (`order` becomes move | attack)

**Systems** (sim, deterministic; tick order):

1. **`playerControl`:** input becomes intent. It applies the WASD/MOBA target rules above, chases in MOBA, and queues ability presses. While a rooting cast runs, the desired velocity is 0.
2. **`casting`:** generic, for any caster. It ticks cooldowns, starts the AA when the target is in range and the slot is ready (or a queued ability when it can start), and advances the windup → channel phases. It applies effects through `applyDamage` and the 2.1 hit shapes.
3. `locomotion`, `patrol`, `dummy`, as before

While a cast runs, the caster's facing turns toward its aim at the movement turn rate, instead of following movement.

**MOBA chase:**

- The cat paths to the target with the existing `findPath`, and re-paths when the target has moved more than 0.25 m from the path's goal.
- It stops once in range and attacks, then chases again if the patrolling dummy walks off.
- Paths ignore solid bodies as before, so you slide round.

**Hover pick (view):**

- Each enemy gets an invisible pick cylinder: its hurtbox radius + 0.15 m, model height. That's cheaper and more forgiving than picking the model's meshes.
- The shell writes the picked uid with `input.setHover(uid | null)` each frame, like `setAim`.
- Replay format: a new optional `HOVER` field bit, so old fixtures stay valid (no hover).

**The AA's look:**

- **Attack clip:** the tiger's `attack` clip is time-scaled so its strike frame lands at the end of the windup. It fades in fast (~0.08 s) over the gait, then fades back out after the strike, sooner if the cat moves. It's driven by `caster.casts` and `cast.elapsed` on `viewTime()`.
- **Hover highlight:** a subtle outline on the hovered enemy. A `hoveredEnemy` signal swaps the cursor for an attack cursor (CSS cursor in WASD, the software cursor in MOBA).
- **Target ring:** a thin red ring under the current target in the overlay scene, hidden when there's none.
- **Debug draw:** a new `abilities` category (starts hidden) shows:
  - the AA range ring round the cat
  - a line to the target, green when it's in range
  - the cast phase and cooldown as text
  - effect shapes as they land
- **Cheat:** `noCooldowns`, a sim command (`systems/cheats.ts`) for testing.

## Architecture

```
data/abilities.ts      ability definitions (Babylon-free); the Cat's slots: primary = catAA
combat/abilities.ts    pure helpers: range check (edge to edge), aim resolution, effect application
systems/casting.ts     generic caster system: cooldowns, buffer, cast phases, effects
systems/playerControl  + WASD/MOBA target rules, MOBA chase, ability presses → caster.queued
ecs/world.ts           + uid, faction, caster; Player.order: move | attack
input/                 InputFrame.hover (uid | null), setHover; mmo: primary = Mouse0, interact = KeyF
replay/format.ts       + HOVER field bit (optional, old files stay valid)
render/                pick cylinders + hover outline (dummies.ts), target ring, attack clip (tiger.ts)
ui/                    attack cursor from a hoveredEnemy signal
```

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] WASD: left-clicking a dummy auto-attacks it at 0.7/s whenever it's in range, and keeps going after release until a WASD movement or another click. Holding A + left click over a dummy moves left, attacks it when ready and in range, and keeps moving. Hovering another dummy while holding switches the target.
- [ ] MOBA: right-clicking a dummy chases it into range and keeps attacking, including the patrolling one. Right-clicking the ground moves, and `S` stops. Holding right click follows the cursor's target or ground.
- [ ] The cat is rooted only during the windup, faces its target, and every started attack lands.
- [ ] Hovered enemies are outlined with an attack cursor, the target has a ring, the attack clip lines up with the hit, and damage numbers pop.
- [ ] The framework is Babylon-free and deterministic. Unit tests cover instant, windup and channel casts, cooldowns, the buffer, the aim kinds, shape effects and a non-player caster.
- [ ] Existing fixtures pass (checkpoints rewritten only for the new components), the new `yard-auto-attack` fixture passes, and `pnpm check` passes.
- [ ] Docs are updated, the devlog entry is written, 2.2 is ticked, and the PR is open with the checks and Preview green.
