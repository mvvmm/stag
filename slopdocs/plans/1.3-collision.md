# 1.3 Collision: Implementation Plan

> Status: **done (2026-09-27).** Parent: [implementation-plan.md](../implementation-plan.md) step 1.3.

## Goal

The player stops walking through walls:

- **Swept collision** of the player's circle against every obstacle, grown by the player's radius (the same shapes pathing uses), so it can't tunnel at any speed. Dashes, pounces and knockback in 2.x get this for free.
- **Projected sliding:** pushing into a wall at an angle slides along it at the speed of the motion's component along the wall. Pushing straight in stops you. Box corners are rounded by the growth, so the player rounds them smoothly.
- **Test and debug aids:** a `collision` debug-draw category, a noclip cheat that replays, a collision gym room full of nasty cases, and a recorded replay fixture.

When it's done, WASD can't get through or into any obstacle in either room. Walls feel solid, and sliding along them is smooth, with no jitter or sticking in corners or wedges. Click-to-move still reaches its goals. `pnpm check` must pass.

Out of scope:

- mover-vs-mover collision and separation (enemies, 3.x). The code works for any `mover`, so enemies only add that part.
- dashes and knockback (2.x), which will reuse `moveAndSlide`
- per-obstacle flags such as "low walls block walking but not projectiles" or "a pounce jumps over them". For now every obstacle blocks walking, and `height` stays view and line-of-sight data.
- pits and hazards, and any dynamic obstacle that moves
- a spatial broadphase. A room has ~20 obstacles, so a bounds check per obstacle is enough.
- the camera (1.4)

## Decisions

| Area | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Slide feel | **Projected** | The blocked part of the motion is dropped and the part along the surface is kept: a 45° push slides at ~71% speed, and a head-on push stops. Hades and Diablo do this. It's predictable and reads as solid. |
| Algorithm | **Swept + slide** | Each tick, cast the circle along its motion against the grown shapes, stop just before the first hit, project what's left onto the surface, and cast again, at most 4 times. A final overlap check pushes the circle out if something went wrong, which should never happen. It can't tunnel at any speed. |
| Path corners | **Collision slides them** | No pathing change. Steering at full speed can cut a grown corner by up to ~0.1 m. With collision on, the player grazes the corner, slides round its rounded edge and loses a little speed for a tick or two. We check in play whether that dip shows. If it does, the fallback is a small pathing margin or steering tighter at corners. |
| Debug aids | **All four** | A `collision` draw category, a noclip command (`sim: true`, recorded), a collision gym room as its own scene, and an `arena-collision` replay fixture. |

### Defaults I picked (object if you disagree)

- **Geometry** (`collision/`, new, Babylon-free, pure and tested):
  - `collision/cast.ts`: `castCircle(from, motion, shape, radius)` returns the first time of impact `t ∈ [0, 1]` and the surface normal there, or `null`. It's a ray against the grown shape, done exactly:
    - circle: a ray against a circle of `r + radius`
    - box: in the box's local frame, a ray against the rounded rectangle, which is the four sides pushed out by `radius` plus a circle of `radius` at each corner. The earliest hit wins.
    - Starting in contact (within the skin) while moving away from or along the surface (`motion · n ≥ 0`) is not a hit, so a slide along a wall doesn't catch on it.
  - `collision/slide.ts`: `moveAndSlide(obstacles, from, motion, radius)` returns `{ position, contacts, touching, depenetrated }`:
    1. Keep the obstacles whose grown bounds come within `|motion| + radius` of the start (a cheap AABB filter; see the notes for why not the swept bounds).
    2. Up to 4 times: cast against each and take the earliest hit. With no hit, move the whole way and stop. On a hit, move to it minus the **skin** (0.001 m) along the motion, remove the normal component from what's left of the motion (`m -= (m · n) n`), and go again. If the slide goes into another shape the circle is touching, it's wedged: stop.
    3. After the last iteration, drop what's left of the motion. In a wedge that's the right answer, since the two normals cancel the motion out.
    4. Safety net: if the result is closer than `radius - skin` to any shape, `pushOut` from `nav/shapes.ts`, up to 3 passes, and set `depenetrated`.
  - `nav/shapes.ts` stays where it is. `collision/` imports `distanceToShape` and `pushOut` from it.
- **Where it runs:** inside `locomotion`, which is the one place positions integrate. The step `position += v · dt` becomes `moveAndSlide(obstacles, position, v · dt, stats.radius)`. Then the velocity loses its component into every surface it touches at its new position (`v -= min(0, v · n) n`; stopped if that still goes into one, as in a wedge). Sliding therefore carries a real, reduced velocity, and letting go of a wall doesn't feel sticky. With no input, the player just brakes as usual.
  - This applies to any `mover` with a transform. The radius comes from `movementStats(entity).radius`, the same number pathing uses, so the path shapes and collision shapes always match.
  - Obstacles come from `world.with("obstacle")` each tick. There are ~20, and they're plain data.
- **Depenetration on start:** if the player starts a tick overlapping a shape (the `player.radius` tunable just grew, or an entity-pane edit put them inside a wall), a zero-length `moveAndSlide` still runs the safety net and pushes them out, with a red flash in the `collision` category. Spawns are placed so this never happens in normal play.
- **Arrival:** `playerControl`'s landing snap (`position = goal`) stays. Goals are resolved outside the grown shapes plus a 0.02 m skin, so the snap never lands in a wall.
- **Noclip:** a `noclip?: true` tag component on the entity, so snapshots and the entity pane see it. When it's set, `locomotion` integrates without collision.
  - The command `cheats.noclip` (group `Cheats`, in the Gameplay folder, `sim: true`) toggles it on the player.
  - It's the first `sim: true` command, so it needs a Babylon-free home that headless replays can run: `systems/cheats.ts` exports `simCommands: Record<id, (world) => void>`. The debug command calls it, and the replay fixture test passes it as `runCommand`.
  - `HeadlessOptions.runCommand` becomes `(id, world) => void`, and the pane and live replay playback go through the same map.
- **`collision` debug-draw category** (sim side, write-only, starts hidden like every category):
  - each contact of the tick: a small point at the contact on the surface and a short normal arrow (orange)
  - the slid motion when it differs from the wanted one (the wanted motion faint, the result solid)
  - a red circle round the player on a tick that needed the safety net
- **Collision gym** (`data/rooms/gym.ts`, scene id `gym`, label "Collision gym"): a second hand-built room, about 24 × 16 m, with:
  - an acute wedge (two boxes meeting at ~30°) and a V between a rotated box and a wall
  - a corridor that just fits (0.82 m at the default radius of 0.4) and a gap that just doesn't (0.79 m). (The plan expected pathing's 0.25 m grid to miss the 0.82 m corridor; playtesting turned that into a bug, and pathing became exact. See the notes.)
  - a tight pillar cluster with gaps a bit wider and a bit narrower than the player
  - two rotated boxes touching at a corner, and a box whose corner touches a pillar
  - a long diagonal wall to slide along
  - The arena sim becomes a factory, `roomSim(id, label, room)`. `arena` (greybox) and `gym` share it, and they share the view too (`roomScene(sim)` in `arena.view.ts`). Both are listed in `sims.ts` and `index.ts`.
- **Tests**:
  - `castCircle`: head-on and grazing hits on circles and on the sides and corners of rotated boxes, misses, tangent motion, zero motion, starting in contact moving along or away, and starting in contact moving in.
  - `moveAndSlide`: projected slide speeds (45° gives cos 45 of the motion), head-on stops, stops in wedges and V's, rounding a box corner, no tunneling through the thinnest wall at 1000 m/s, the just-fits corridor passes, and the just-too-narrow gap doesn't.
  - A **property test**: seeded random walks with random direction changes and speeds up to 50 m/s, 20k ticks each in the gym and the greybox. The player never ends a tick closer than `radius - skin` to a shape, and the safety net never fires.
  - `locomotion`: velocity into a wall is clipped, sliding speed is the projection, and noclip goes through.
  - dmath guard: `collision/` joins `SIM_PATHS`. Rule 1's list gets `collision/` too.
- **Budget:** `moveAndSlide` for the player against the greybox room takes < 20 µs a tick in Node on average. That's checked loosely, like the path budget.
- **Replays:**
  - `arena-walk` and `arena-paths` keep their recorded input, and `pnpm replay:update` rewrites their checkpoints. `arena-walk` walked through walls, so its path changes.
  - A new **`arena-collision`** fixture, recorded in the browser: WASD along and into walls, into corners, round pillars and through the tight gap, then click-to-move grazing corners, plus a noclip toggle through a wall and back out (the depenetration case). A gym fixture is optional if something there turns out to be fragile.

## Architecture

```
collision/            (new, Babylon-free)
  cast.ts             castCircle: swept circle vs grown circle / rounded box → { t, normal }            (tested)
  slide.ts            moveAndSlide: iterate casts, project, skin, safety-net pushOut                    (tested)
nav/shapes.ts         unchanged; collision imports distanceToShape, pushOut
ecs/world.ts          + noclip?: true
systems/
  locomotion.ts       integration goes through moveAndSlide, velocity clipped by contact normals, debug draw (tested)
  cheats.ts           simCommands (noclip), Babylon-free so headless replays can run them
data/rooms/gym.ts     the collision gym room
scenes/arena.ts       roomSim(id, label, room); arena + gym
scenes/arena.view.ts  roomScene(sim): shared view for both rooms
scenes/sims.ts, index.ts  + gym
debug/                cheats.noclip command (sim: true, via simCommands)
replay/headless.ts    runCommand(id, world)
replay/fixtures/      arena-walk, arena-paths updated; + arena-collision
```

Rule 1 holds: `collision/`, `systems/`, `data/` and `scenes/arena.ts` import neither Babylon nor Preact, and `collision/` uses only `dmath` for hypot (rule 6).

## Implementation notes

- **Verified in headless Chrome** (agent-browser, real WebGPU) on the dev server:
  - Walking south into the arena's south wall stopped at z −9.599 (the face plus the radius plus the skin) with the velocity zeroed. Adding D slid along it at 4.95 m/s, which is exactly 7 · cos 45°.
  - In the gym, the 0.82 m corridor let the player through, and the 0.79 m corridor and the 0.7 m pillar gap stopped them. Pushing west into the ±15° wedge stopped at x −7.49, where a 0.4 m circle fits between the arms.
  - Noclip from the pane walked through the south wall and out of the room, and a recording with both toggles played back in the browser without divergence. `arena-collision`, `arena-paths` and `arena-walk` all play to the end in Chrome without divergence too.
- **The property test found a real bug straight away.** The broadphase kept only the shapes near the straight-line motion's bounds, but a slide can turn sideways out of those bounds and into a shape that was filtered out. The safety net used the same list, so it didn't catch it either. A slide never lengthens the motion, so the filter now keeps everything within `|motion| + radius` of the start. After the fix, a soak run (2 rooms × 4 radii × 20 seeds × 20k ticks, up to 50 m/s) never overlapped or needed the safety net. The committed test runs 2 radii and 1 seed per room.
- **The wedge check uses what's touching now, not the tick's earlier hits.** The first version stopped a slide when it pointed into any normal hit earlier in the tick. Sliding round a pillar broke that: the earlier normals on the same curved surface point "into" the new slide direction once you're past them. The check now looks only at *other* shapes the circle is touching at its current position, with their normals there, and `clipVelocity` works the same way at the end position.
- **The skin backs off along the motion, not along the normal.** The path up to a hit is clear of everything, so stepping back along it can't push the circle into a neighbour, which it could in a wedge when it stepped back along the normal. Grazing hits leave less than the full skin, which is fine: a start within 1e-9 of a surface counts as contact, and motion that's tangent up to rounding isn't "into" it.
- **Sliding speed ramps up to the projection instead of jumping to it.** The velocity is clipped each tick and then accelerates toward the desired velocity again, so the along-wall speed closes in on `speed · cos θ` over a few ticks (within 1e-5 after a second). It feels natural, and the test allows for it.
- **Corner dips aren't collision.** In `arena-collision` the worst mid-path slowdown was 7 → 3.8 m/s for ~10 ticks. Replaying the same input with collision off gives identical speeds to the hundredth: it's `locomotion` steering through a sharp path corner (from 1.2), not a graze. Click-to-move never got stuck (at most one tick under 1 m/s while following an order), and all 7 orders in `arena-paths` still arrive.
- **Noclip can leave the room.** Turned off outside the perimeter, the player stays outside (colliding with the wall's outer face). That's fine for a cheat. The fixture's noclip leg is short, so it stops inside the wall and gets pushed back into the room.
- **The fixture was scripted, not hand-played.** `arena-collision` (2056 ticks) was driven by keyboard and pointer events from agent-browser: 936 ticks touching a wall, 41 in a wedge, the noclip push-out, and 6 right-clicks around the room.
- **First `sim: true` command.** `HeadlessOptions.runCommand` and `applyEvent`'s runner now get the world. `runReplay` defaults to `simCommands`, so the fixture test needs no setup. The pane shows noclip as a Gameplay checkbox that runs the command, rather than as a button.
- **Pathing became exact (after the first playtest).** You found you could walk into the gym's pillar pocket with WASD but not click in or out: its only way in is the 0.836 m gap, which leaves 3.6 cm of free band for the player's center, and the 0.25 m nav grid had no cell center in it. You chose to replace the grid (from 1.2) with an **exact tangent graph** over the same grown shapes collision uses, rather than patching the grid with gap portals or shrinking the cells:
  - `nav/graph.ts`: pillars grow into circles and box corners into circles of the player radius (a box's sides are the tangents between its corner circles). Nodes are tangent points with a direction round their circle; edges are the tangent segments that pass the exact segment test (within 1e-6 m) and the arcs between neighbouring nodes that a per-circle sample mask says are free. Built in 4–7 ms per room and radius, cached per world like the grid was.
  - `nav/path.ts`: a query adds tangents from the start and to the goal, runs A* (`nav/astar.ts`, now generic with the same deterministic tie-breaking), and turns arcs into a polygon whose sides touch the circle and whose corners stick out at most 1 mm. Paths are optimal as found, so string pulling is gone. Queries average 0.06 ms (0.27 ms worst), so holding the button now simply repaths whenever the cursor moves (the in-cell shortcut had also kept stale detours once the goal moved into the open).
  - Unreachable goals: from the start and the 16 reachable nodes closest to the goal, the body slides toward it with `moveAndSlide`, and the path ends at the closest straight-line result (so a click behind a wall stops at the wall, not at a graph node).
  - Tests: every path between random free points in both rooms can be walked with `moveAndSlide` to its goal; the pocket is reachable (and leavable) at radius 0.4 and not at 0.43; the 0.82 m corridor is used and the 0.79 m one isn't; a click into the pocket and back through `playerControl` lands exactly. The `navgrid` debug category became `navgraph` (the grown outlines), and the graph's tangents got their own `navedges` toggle. A new `gym-gaps` fixture (clicks into and out of the pocket, WASD in then click out, both corridors, the wedge) was scripted in the browser; all four fixtures replay in Node and Chrome, and `arena-paths` still has 7 of 8 clicks arriving (the 8th was replaced by a new click).
- **Not yet played by hand.** Everything above is automated or scripted. The feel of sliding, the corner dips and the gym are for you to try on the Preview.
